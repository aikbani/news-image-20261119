A one-image front page summing up the world's news of Thursday 19 November 2026:
**50 stories from 7 regions** — North America, South America, Europe, Africa,
the Middle East, Asia-Pacific and Global — led by the Trump–Xi–Putin summit in
Shenzhen, the ICJ ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya,
and Anthropic's record $81bn IPO on the same day GTA VI launched.

The poster also carries a drawn world map with story locations, a UTC timeline
of the day, and a DATE CHECK box: every story happened or was first reported
on 19 November 2026, each tagged `reported`, `claim` or `forecast`.

## Run

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
# writes out/entry.png (byte-identical to entry.png in this repo)
```

`main.py` is Python 3.13 standard library only and needs no network; it simply
copies the pre-rendered `entry.png` (1024×1024 PNG) to the output directory.
